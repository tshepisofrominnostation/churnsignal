Read this before you trust a number

  • The data is synthetic. 3,600 messages written from templates modelled on public South African telecom complaint themes. Not scraped from Hellopeter, X or any customer. Real accuracy will be lower.
  • Labels are generated, not human-annotated. That is why precision and recall can be reported, and why a pilot on real labelled messages is the first next step.
  • Leakage is controlled. The split is made on unique normalised text, so no message appears in both training and testing.
  • Provider comparisons are not findings. Provider was assigned at random, so differences between operators are noise.
  • English-dominant. A few code-switched examples only; isiZulu, isiXhosa and Afrikaans coverage is minimal.
  • It triages; it does not judge. It ranks who a human should reply to first.
