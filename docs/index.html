Read this before you trust a number

  • The data is synthetic. 3,600 messages written from templates modelled on public themes in South African telecom complaints. It is not scraped from Hellopeter, X or any customer. Real-world accuracy will be lower.
  • Labels are generated, not annotated by people. That is why precision and recall can be reported at all, and also why a pilot on real, human-labelled messages is the first next step.
  • Provider comparisons are not findings. Provider was assigned at random in the generator, so any difference between operators here is noise.
  • English-dominant. A few code-switched examples are included; isiZulu, isiXhosa and Afrikaans coverage is minimal.
  • Triage, not a verdict. It ranks who a human should reply to first. It does not decide anything about a customer.
